# Sources and credits

## Story
| Use | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| Plot skeleton of Episode 1 (`docs/content/scenes/ep1-script.json`) | Miguel de Cervantes, *Don Quixote*, Part I, ch. VII (departure with Sancho, the promised island) and ch. VIII (the windmills, Frestón, the road to Puerto Lápice), English translation by John Ormsby, 1885 | Public domain (translation published 1885; Project Gutenberg eBook #996) |

All dialogue, jokes, scene-setting lines, bridge lines, recaps and compressed endings are new writing for RunX. A few lines paraphrase Ormsby's public-domain text ("go aside and say your prayers", "fly not, cowards"). Nothing is taken from modern translations, films or musicals.

Details that do not appear in ch. VII–VIII and were checked against the content rule (no new events, characters or places): the heat on the plain (scene-setting only), Sancho leaving before his wife wakes (a joke, ch. VII says he left without taking leave), a mill grinding for "half the valley" (a joke), Dulcinea of El Toboso (named in earlier chapters).
